# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in Map Tunisia a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- respecting differing viewpoints, experiences and languages (Arabic, French, English and Tunisian Derja are all welcome here);
- giving constructive feedback, and accepting it gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community and for the accuracy of the map;
- showing empathy and kindness toward other people.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Discussions about borders and place names should stay factual and cite sources.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents the
project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@Alaa-Taieb](https://github.com/Alaa-Taieb),
or through GitHub's *Report content* feature. Every report is reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and
the reporter's privacy is respected.

Depending on how severe or repeated the behaviour is, consequences range from a private warning, through a
temporary ban, to a permanent ban from the project.
